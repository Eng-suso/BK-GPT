# Process Discovery

## Procedure

1. Define the process scope.
2. Identify trigger, start, end and expected outcome.
3. Identify stakeholders and their perspective.
4. List available evidence: interviews, documents, systems, examples and observations.
5. Separate official process from actual process.
6. Separate evidence from inference.
7. Extract actors, activities, decisions, handoffs, systems, data, exceptions and controls.
8. Detect contradictions and missing knowledge.
9. Decide the next best stakeholder or source to collect.
10. Assess discovery readiness before modeling.

## Rules

Do not create a confirmed As-Is model from one weak source.
Do not convert plausible assumptions into confirmed facts.
Always preserve source attribution for important process claims.
