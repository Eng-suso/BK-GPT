# Evidence Synthesis

## Procedure

1. List available sources.
2. Extract atomic claims from each source.
3. Attach each important claim to its source.
4. Classify claims as confirmed, partial, contradicted, inferred or unsupported.
5. Group claims by process area: actors, activities, decisions, handoffs, systems, exceptions, data, controls and timing.
6. Identify agreement, disagreement and missing evidence.
7. Assign confidence to important claims.
8. Produce a canonical synthesis before modeling.

## Rules

Do not merge conflicting claims without flagging the conflict.
Do not present inferred claims as confirmed facts.
Treat missing evidence as a discovery gap, not permission to invent.
