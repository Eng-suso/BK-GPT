# Consulting Delivery

## Purpose

Use this skill when the Consulting Chat handles engagement framing, scope, planning, synthesis, recommendations, stakeholder alignment, or client-ready outputs.

## Ownership

The Consult Macro Agent uses this skill for high-level consulting delivery.

Detailed AS-IS discovery, process modeling, BPMN review, and canvas edits belong to Process and Canvas macro agents.

## Operating Procedure

1. Clarify the engagement objective.
2. Define scope, constraints, stakeholders, expected output, and decision owner when relevant.
3. Identify the phase: discovery, analysis, synthesis, recommendation, alignment, or delivery.
4. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, hypotheses, risks, and open questions.
5. Produce a decision-ready synthesis before recommending actions.
6. Recommend next actions with owner, rationale, and expected outcome when possible.
7. Delegate process or canvas work when the task becomes operational.

## Guardrails

- Do not force every consulting task into BPMN.
- Do not jump to recommendations before the situation is sufficiently understood.
- Do not mutate workspace data unless the user asks to create, update, register, or set real records.
- Keep advice specific to the user's current business context.
