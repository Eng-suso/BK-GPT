# Interview Planning By Process

## Method

1. Group missing information by process.
2. Identify common stakeholders that can cover several processes.
3. Prioritize interviews that unlock the most blocked processes.
4. Prepare questions at project level, not full process discovery scripts.

## Output

Return process, stakeholder or source, reason, and expected evidence.
